## Rules, in short

- **Question:** Do the names Claude picks from the news right after the US open go up over the next 1, 5 and 20 US trading days, and by how much (also vs QQQ / BTC-USD)?
- **When:** one run each US trading day right after the open (New York 09:30-16:00). Up to 3 picks (zero is allowed), from 2026-11-09 to 2027-02-06; results until 2027-03-10.
- **Allowed:** Stocks/ETFs listed on a US exchange (Nasdaq, NYSE, NYSE American, NYSE Arca, Cboe) with last close > $1; non-stablecoin crypto in the top 250 by market cap.
- **Not allowed:** OTC/pink sheets, foreign-only listings, coins outside the top 250, stablecoins, any symbol picked in the last 20 US trading days.
- **Entry price:** Price at pick time: close of the last 1-minute Yahoo bar that starts at or before the moment the pick is made (at most 10 minutes earlier), for the pick and its benchmark.
- **Results:** Official close on the 1st, 5th and 20th US trading day after the pick day (crypto: UTC daily close on those dates). US trading days = dates on which QQQ has a daily bar.
- **Benchmark:** QQQ for stocks, BTC-USD for crypto, measured over the same time span as the pick.
- **Prices:** Never invent a price. Missing stays NULL with a note and is retried. After 3 failed runs a value is marked "not covered by price feed". Paper trading only: no orders are ever placed.
